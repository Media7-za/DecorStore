Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay for my order?

Potanical make use of Payfast as our preferred payment gateway. Not only is this one of the most secure payment gateways but it also provides you with multiple payment options.

How does delivery work?

We offer Express or Standard delivery straight to your door or alternatively, your order can be collected at our flagship store in Flamingo Shopping Centre, Modderfontein, JHB.

Can I view the products in person?

Absolutely! We have a shop located in Flamingo Shopping centre in Modderfontein. Open 7 days a week. 

How often do you have new designs?

We will be renewing our collection every 6 months and/or as trends change. Our ultimate aim is to bring you the most beautifully crafted, unique and up to date designs.

Do you keep stock of all your pots?

Yes, we keep stock based on all the styles available on our website. Should that style die down, we automatically renew it with the new collection. However, all the pots displayed on the website are ready to ship on completion of order.

What pots size is right for me?
In the event that you cant be sure of what size to get, please check out our sizing guide or speak to our team who will all gladly assist you.
